How a parent-run school actually works

A practical conversation with people doing the work at Creekside Cooperative Preschool & Kindergarten—not a polished panel and not a sales webinar.

Why this exists

Stop rebuilding the same knowledge every year.

Co-op families inherit real operational responsibility: board roles, budgets, membership, websites, fundraising, classroom participation, parent education, facilities, and yearly handoffs. This session is a test of a simple idea: make that operational knowledge useful outside one school, then let people support the school that shared it.

Draft agenda
  • 0–10
    What a co-op actually is
    Where parent ownership begins and where teachers/directors lead.
  • 10–25
    The invisible operating system
    Jobs, board handoffs, classroom participation, training, scheduling, and communication.
  • 25–40
    What gets hard
    Fundraising, volunteer energy, continuity, technology, and keeping knowledge from walking out the door.
  • 40–60
    Open office hours
    Bring a real problem from your school or a question about starting one.
