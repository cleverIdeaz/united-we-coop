Learn for free. Share what works. Support the people who helped.

The goal is simple: fewer co-ops reinventing the wheel, and more support flowing between the people keeping them alive.

Who this is for

You do not have to already know how a co-op works.

01

Already in a co-op

Learn how other schools handle boards, family jobs, fundraising, enrollment, handoffs, technology, and the everyday problems that make co-ops hard to run.

02

Trying to improve one

Find real examples, live conversations, and reusable ideas instead of rebuilding every process from scratch.

03

Thinking about starting one

Hear directly from people doing the work and understand what cooperative education actually looks like before you take the leap.

How it works

One co-op shares. Everybody gets stronger.

01

Share

A teacher, parent, board member, treasurer, fundraiser, or volunteer hosts a useful conversation or shares a resource.

02

Learn

People discover it because they have the same question or problem — not because they already know that school.

03

Keep it

The recording, template, or notes stay available so the next person does not have to start from zero.

04

Give back

If the school helped you, support them directly. No pooled fund and no platform cut in this pilot.

Why support Creekside?

Because the school is contributing first.

Creekside is putting its experience on the table so other co-ops and families can learn from it. A donation supports Creekside itself while this pilot tests a bigger idea: useful contribution can create attention, connection, and reciprocal support between co-ops.

If this works

Every co-op can teach something — and every co-op can benefit.

LEARN

Stop starting from zero

Find people who have already handled the role, problem, transition, or decision in front of you.

SHARE

Turn experience into a resource

Host one practical event, share one template, or explain one hard-won lesson. That is enough to help another school.

SUPPORT

Let generosity follow usefulness

When another co-op saves you time, gives you an idea, or helps your family, you have a direct way to support the school that contributed.
