How it works

Three steps. No giant platform required.

01

Share

A co-op hosts one useful conversation or contributes one resource: board handoffs, fundraising, family jobs, enrollment, technology, whatever it knows.

02

Learn

Other co-ops, families, and people thinking about starting one can find it and use it immediately.

03

Give back

If it helped, donate directly to the school that shared it. Then another co-op takes a turn.
