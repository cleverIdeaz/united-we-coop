Why Creekside is starting here

Fundraising should create value, not just asks.

Creekside already depends on family participation and fundraising to support its operating budget, classroom experiences, facilities, curriculum, continuing education, and scholarship opportunities. United We Co-op is an experiment in turning the school's operational knowledge into something other families and co-ops can use—without requiring Creekside families to rely only on personal fundraising circles.

The first test is simple: host a useful public office hour, invite the broader co-op community, keep the recording accessible, and route anyone who wants to help directly to Creekside's existing giving tools.
